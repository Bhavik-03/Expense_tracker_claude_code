Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 27, 2026

1. Data We Collect

When you use Spendly, we collect the information you provide directly, such as your account details (name, email, password) and the expense data you log, including amounts, categories, dates, and descriptions. We may also collect basic technical information, such as browser type and device information, to help us maintain and improve the Service.

2. How We Use Your Data

We use the information we collect to operate and provide the Service to you, including displaying your expense history, generating spending summaries, and maintaining your account. We may also use aggregated, anonymized data to understand usage patterns and improve Spendly's features.

We do not use your personal financial data for advertising purposes.

3. Data Storage

Your data is stored securely and is only accessible to you through your authenticated account. We take reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect your information against unauthorized access, loss, or misuse. However, no method of electronic storage is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

You may request deletion of your account and associated data at any time through the app or by contacting us.

4. Third Party Services

Spendly does not sell your personal or financial data to third parties. We may rely on trusted third-party service providers for functions such as hosting and infrastructure, which may process data on our behalf solely to support the operation of the Service. These providers are only permitted to use your data as necessary to provide their services to us.

5. Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy or how your data is handled, please contact us through the support channels provided within the app. See our Terms and Conditions for more information about your use of the Service.
